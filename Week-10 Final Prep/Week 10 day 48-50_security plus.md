## **⚡ 1\. Light Formula & Quantitative Risk Warm-Up**

Take 10 minutes to review these essential quantitative formulas. Practice calculating them mentally or on scratch paper:

### **Quantitative Risk Formula Summary**

| Metric / Formula | Calculation | Description & Purpose |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Single Loss Expectancy (SLE)** | SLE \= AV × EF | Financial impact resulting from a single security incident. |
| **Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE)** | ALE \= SLE × ARO | Total projected financial cost incurred over the course of a full year. |
| **Exposure Factor (EF)** | EF \= % Loss | Percentage of total asset value compromised during an attack. |
| **Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO)** | ARO \= Occurrences / Year | Estimated frequency of a specific incident occurring within a one-year period. |

> **Calculation Practice:** **Example Calculation:** For an asset valued at $100,000 with an Exposure Factor (EF) of 20%, the Single Loss Expectancy (SLE) is $20,000 ($100,000 × 0.20). With an Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO) of 0.5 (occurring once every 2 years), the resulting Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE) equals $10,000 annually ($20,000 × 0.5).
